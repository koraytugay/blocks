How to use 3D Block Builder

Building

Add Blocks: Hover over any exposed face of a 3D block. A ghost block preview will indicate where the new cube will snap. Left click to attach!

Delete Blocks: Hold Shift and click any block to remove it, or switch to the Delete tool in the top bar.

Paint Blocks: Select a color from the left palette and switch to the Paint tool to recolor existing blocks.

Architecture 2D Views & Orientation

3D Ground Compass Arrows: Look at the 3D ground plane around your blocks! Color-coded arrows and badges point at each side of the structure: ▲ FRONT (Blue), ▲ BACK (Purple), ▲ LEFT (Amber), and ▲ RIGHT (Emerald). Click any arrow to immediately switch to that view!

Interactive 3D ViewCube: The orientation cube in the top-right corner rotates in real-time with the scene. Click any face on the ViewCube to snap flat to that view, or drag it directly to orbit!

Hover Previews: Hovering your mouse over any 2D view button or ViewCube face will highlight that side's arrow in the 3D scene before you click.

Orthogonal Projections: Click TOP, BOTTOM, FRONT, BACK, LEFT, or RIGHT to view true architectural projections without perspective distortion. Toggle X-Ray to see through upper blocks, or Height Labels to see vertical layers.

3D Camera Controls

Rotate: Click and drag anywhere with the mouse to orbit around your creation in full 3D.

Zoom: Use mouse scroll wheel, trackpad pinch, or the on-screen +/- buttons to zoom smoothly.

Pan: Hold Shift and drag, or drag with the Right Mouse Button to pan around the scene.

Reset / Recenter: Press R or click the recenter icon to center the camera on your creation.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • 1 Add Tool
  • 2 Delete Tool
  • 3 Paint Tool
  • X Toggle X-Ray Mode
  • H Toggle Height Labels
  • Ctrl+Z Undo
  • Ctrl+Y Redo
  • Space Auto-Rotate
  • R Recenter Camera
